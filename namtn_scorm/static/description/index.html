NAMTN SCORM Integration for Odoo eLearning

Enhance Odoo eLearning (Website Slides) with full SCORM 1.2 & 2004 support. Upload, track, and manage SCORM courses seamlessly.

1. How to setup:
  • Go to eLearning → Courses.
  • Create a new course or edit an existing one.
  • Click "Upload SCORM" to import your SCORM package (.zip).
  • Publish the course for learners to access.

Note: Ensure your SCORM packages are valid. SCORM 1.2 and 2004 are supported.

2. Features:
  • Upload and import SCORM packages (.zip).
  • Track learner progress and completion status automatically.
  • Full-screen player mode for immersive learning.
  • Portal rating and feedback support.
  • Secure access rights and role management.
